# OreLens — Product Requirements

## Product

OreLens is a commodity and company intelligence product for the Sectors hackathon. It connects commodity drivers, company exposure, resilience, evidence, and scenarios in one analysis flow.

## MVP user experience

The MVP should support these views:

- Commodity Overview
- Commodity Driver Map
- Company Comparison
- Company Detail
- Exposure and Resilience breakdown
- Evidence View
- Scenario UI

## Product boundaries

- The product may analyze commodity and company relationships.
- Scenario inputs may be used to explore analysis outcomes.
- The MVP must not present the unfinished scoring model as final.
- The product must not become a stock-price prediction product.

## Success condition

Users can move from a commodity overview to its drivers, compare related companies, inspect exposure and resilience, review evidence, and explore a scenario using normalized data.
